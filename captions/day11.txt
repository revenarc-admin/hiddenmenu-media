Where does your tip actually go? It's not always just your server.

Your tip often gets shared with bussers, bartenders and runners. Under federal law, managers and supervisors can't keep any of it. And an automatic 18% service charge isn't legally a tip at all.

Sources: US Department of Labor, dol.gov/agencies/whd/flsa/tips ; IRS, irs.gov/taxtopics/tc761 . State rules vary.
