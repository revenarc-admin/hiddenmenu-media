Who to tip at a hotel, and exactly how much. US etiquette, in one list.

Housekeeping $2 to $5 a day, left daily with a note. Bellhop $2 for the first bag and $1 for each extra. Valet $2 to $5 when the car comes back. Concierge $5 to $10 for reservations or tickets.

Source: Emily Post Institute, emilypost.com/advice/general-tipping-guide (US guidelines)
