Why fancy restaurants hide the dollar sign. A Cornell study measured what it does to your bill.

Researchers at Cornell and the Culinary Institute of America handed lunch guests one of three menus at random. The guests whose menu had no dollar signs spent about 8% more.

A bare "32" on a menu is still $32. Read it that way.
