Surprise resort fees just lost their hiding spot. Here's the rule that changed it.

Surprise resort fees are on their way out. Since May 12, 2025, the FTC requires hotels and booking sites in the US to show the total price, including mandatory fees, upfront. Taxes and optional extras can still be added later.

Source: FTC, ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions
