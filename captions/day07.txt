Look closely at the tip screen before you tap 25%. It may be doing math you didn't ask for.

That tablet with the 20, 25 and 30% buttons? Some of them calculate on the total after tax. The Emily Post Institute says tipping on the pre-tax amount is acceptable and common, and 15 to 20% is the standard for sit-down service.

Sources: Emily Post Institute, emilypost.com/advice/general-tipping-guide and emilypost.com/advice/finer-points-of-tipping
