Researchers had people give real gifts to people in their lives. Some were things to keep, some were things to do. The things to do brought people closer, even when the giver didn't come along.

For someone who loves eating out, that's easy: a dinner on you, or a gift card to the place they already go.

Write a date on it. "We should get dinner" never happens.
