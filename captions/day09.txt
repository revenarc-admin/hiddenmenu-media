The second-cheapest wine is not a rip-off. Economists checked 6,335 bottles.

The internet says the second-cheapest wine is the biggest rip-off. Economists tested 6,335 wines on London lists and found the opposite: its markup was lower than the next four bottles up. Markups peaked around the middle of the list.

Source: de Meza and Pathania, LSE Research Online, researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/110874
