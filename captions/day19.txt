Do restaurants reuse the bread basket? Under the FDA Food Code, food that's already been served to a guest can't be served to someone else. Sealed packages and closed bottles are the exceptions.

Source: FDA Food Code 2022, section 3-306.14, fda.gov/food/retail-food-protection/fda-food-code . States adopt the code with their own changes.
