A $100 gift card doesn't cover a $100 dinner.

Add sales tax and a 20% tip and it's closer to $130, and your friend pays the difference. If you're giving a dinner, take the menu price and multiply by about 1.3. Leftover balance just means a coffee there later.

Also ask whether the card can cover the tip. Some restaurants won't allow it.

Or skip the card. Write "dinner is on me" with a date on it, and go with them.
