The famous menu "golden triangle" is a myth. One eye tracker proved it.

For decades, restaurants were told to put their priciest dishes in the top-right "sweet spot" of the menu. It was repeated for decades before anyone tested it.

When researcher Sybil Yang actually tracked diners' eyes in 2012, they read menus like a book: top to bottom, in order. No sweet spot.

What really gets noticed: the first and last items in each section.
