Before you put down a deposit, ask four things: 1. Is there a food and drink minimum, and what counts toward it? 2. Is a service charge added automatically? How much, and does it go to the staff? 3. Set menu or the regular menu? 4. When do they need a final headcount, and do you pay for no-shows? An automatic service charge isn't legally a tip, so it may not all reach the servers.
