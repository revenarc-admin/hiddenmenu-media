The $50 card is covered by federal law. It can't expire for at least five years, and inactivity fees can't start until it's gone a year unused. The $10 bonus card usually isn't covered. Promo cards can expire much sooner and often have blackout days. They have to say so on the front, so look before you plan a dinner around it.
