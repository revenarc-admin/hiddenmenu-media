A lot of holiday dinners get booked this week. Before you put down a deposit, ask four things:

1. Is there a food and drink minimum, and what counts toward it?
2. Is a service charge added automatically? How much, and does it go to the staff?
3. Set menu or the regular menu?
4. When do they need a final headcount, and do you pay for no-shows?

An automatic service charge isn't legally a tip, so it may not all reach the servers. If you want them to get something, ask, or leave a little extra.
