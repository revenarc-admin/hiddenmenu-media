Around 7:15 on a Saturday, almost every table in a restaurant turns over at once. Early diners pay, reservations walk in, the kitchen gets twenty tickets in ten minutes.

Book 6:00 or 8:30 instead. Same food, same menu, and the staff actually has time for you.
